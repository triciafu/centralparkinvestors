SIGNED PLANS VS. INSTALLED CONDITIONS

Following installation, the Customer documented the conditions below, which differ from the signed plans dated October 16, 2025 (the “Plans”) and the Contract. The table compares each specification with the installed condition. Impact ratings are the Customer’s own assessment.

Several conditions involve measurable dimensional differences, impaired function, and reported safety-related concerns, and could not be corrected through routine adjustment.

CONDITIONS NOT CORRECTABLE BY ADJUSTMENT

Several conditions could not be corrected through routine service adjustment. On January 30, 2026, the Company’s Sales Manager concluded, following an on-site review, that the excessive gaps and the blocked outlets could not be addressed without removal (Record Ref. G2). These include:

  1. Obstructed outlet access
  2. Dimensional gap conditions
  3. Oversized filler requirements
  4. Base alignment issues
  5. Concealment deficiencies

REPORTED ELECTRICAL AND SAFETY CONDITIONS

The following conditions were reported to the Company beginning in November 2025 (Exhibit D). A licensed electrician’s written assessment dated June 19, 2026 addresses the outlet access and lighting items (Record Ref. B4):

  1. Obstructed access to electrical infrastructure
  2. Exposed surge protectors and wiring components
  3. Unintended lighting activation
  4. Flickering lighting behavior
  5. Excess heat generation near stored contents
  6. Hardware failures affecting normal operation
