CHRONOLOGY OF NOTICE, CURE OPPORTUNITIES, AND FAILED RESOLUTION

This chronology documents repeated notice of installation defects, multiple coordinated repair efforts, and sustained customer cooperation over several months. Despite confirmed conditions, in-person inspections, and escalation to management and executive leadership, complete remediation was not achieved. The record reflects a pattern of acknowledged defects, partial or delayed corrective efforts, and ultimately a failure to resolve the reported conditions after multiple opportunities to cure.

COOPERATION AND OPPORTUNITY TO CURE

The communication history reflects continued customer cooperation, including scheduling flexibility, access coordination for inspections and repair visits, and ongoing participation in remediation efforts. Multiple opportunities were provided for the company to correct the identified conditions prior to escalation.

SIGNIFICANCE

This timeline reflects repeated notice, confirmed defects, and a failure to achieve complete remediation despite escalation across multiple levels of the organization. It further reflects continued collection activity during an unresolved defect dispute, followed by revised resolution terms inconsistent with prior written assurances.
