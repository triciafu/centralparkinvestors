California Closets - Exhibit A

WRITTEN LIABILITY ASSURANCES AND SUBSEQUENT REVERSAL

Prior to contract execution, written assurances were provided by California Closets that the company would be responsible for damages directly caused during installation, including damage involving concealed utilities or infrastructure behind walls.

After installation conditions were identified that required full removal to properly correct, revised resolution terms were proposed disclaiming responsibility for wall repair, repainting, and restoration associated with removal.

Those revised terms were declined as inconsistent with the prior written assurances. The previously offered removal/refund resolution was later withdrawn, and requests for final payment resumed. This sequence contributed significantly to escalation of the dispute.
