California Closets - Exhibit C

PRE-SALE REPRESENTATIONS VS POST-SALE RESULTS

California Closets publicly represents that its products and services provide custom-fit design solutions, professional installation standards, post-installation support, and long-term warranty protection.

Documented project outcomes were materially inconsistent with several of those representations. Observed conditions included dimensional non-conformance, omitted design-process deliverables, installation protection deficiencies, unresolved post-installation defects, early hardware and electrical failures, and incomplete warranty remediation efforts.

This exhibit summarizes material inconsistencies between public-facing representations and the delivered installation outcome.
