APPROVED SPECIFICATIONS VS. INSTALLED CONDITIONS

Post-installation inspection identified multiple conditions materially inconsistent with the approved design, measurements, and functional specifications. Observed conditions included obstructed electrical access, dimensional non-conformance, hardware operation failures, concealment deficiencies, and visible finish deviations.

The observed conditions were not limited to routine punch-list items. Several involved measurable non-conformance, impaired functionality, reported safety-related concerns, and conditions not reasonably correctable through ordinary adjustment.

Taken together, the installed result reflected systemic execution deficiencies requiring substantial rework, partial removal, or replacement to achieve conformity with the approved project scope.

CONDITIONS REQUIRE REWORK OR REMOVAL

Several observed conditions were not reasonably correctable through routine service adjustment alone. Proper remediation required removal, reconstruction, replacement, or redesign rather than standard post-installation adjustment. These included:
1. Obstructed outlet access
2. Dimensional gap conditions
3. Oversized filler requirements
4. Base alignment issues
5. Concealment deficiencies

ELECTRICAL AND SAFETY CONDITIONS

These conditions warranted timely inspection and corrective remediation. Reported post-installation conditions included:
1. Obstructed access to electrical infrastructure
2. Exposed surge protectors and wiring components
3. Unintended lighting activation
4. Flickering lighting behavior
5. Excess heat generation near stored contents
6. Hardware failures affecting normal operation

TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT

The combined presence of dimensional non-conformance, obstructed electrical access, exposed components, operational failures, and visible finish deviations is more consistent with systemic field measurement, fabrication, and installation control deficiencies than with isolated cosmetic defects.

The installed result materially deviated from the approved specifications and required substantial corrective work to achieve intended conformity.
