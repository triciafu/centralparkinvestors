Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Central Park Investors (“CPI”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards personal information obtained through the website located at centralparkinvestors.com (the “Site”).

By accessing or using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Site, including public webpages and restricted areas used in connection with administrative operations, tenant services, document exchange, payments, communications, investor and strategic partner portals.

It does not apply to information collected offline or through third-party platforms not controlled by CPI, except where such information is integrated into Site services.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect personal information directly from you and automatically through your use of the Site.

Information you provide may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, government-issued identification, date of birth, banking or payment information, tax information, employment details, lease information, investor information, account credentials, documents you upload, and any information you submit through forms or communications.

Information collected automatically may include your IP address, device type, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, session timing, and general usage patterns.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use personal information for purposes that include operating and maintaining the Site, verifying identity, onboarding tenants and users, processing transactions and payments, providing access to restricted content, facilitating communications, managing accounts, fulfilling legal and regulatory obligations, improving Site functionality, monitoring activity, preventing fraud, and sending administrative or relationship communications where permitted by law.

We do not use personal information for behavioral advertising unless separately disclosed.

4. LEGAL BASES TO PROCESSING

Where required by law, we process personal information based on one or more of the following legal grounds: your consent, the performance of contracts, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate business interests, including security, fraud prevention, and operational efficiency.

5. HOW INFORMATION IS SHARED

We may share personal information with service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting providers, payment processors, identity verification services, legal and accounting advisors, analytics providers, and document management platforms.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or as needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of CPI or others.

In the event of a merger, sale, restructuring, or similar business transfer, personal information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

We do not sell personal information.

6. PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL DATA

Payment information may be processed through third-party payment providers. CPI does not store full credit card or consumer payment credentials where processing is handled by an authorized provider.

You acknowledge that payment processing is subject to the privacy policies and terms of those providers.

7. INVESTOR AND TENANT INFORMATION

Information collected in relation to investment or tenant activities may include sensitive financial and contractual information and may be retained as required for compliance, contractual obligations, and ongoing management.

Such information is used solely for legitimate business purposes and maintained in accordance with applicable laws.

8. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, support authentication, analyze usage, and enhance security.

You may adjust browser settings to block cookies, but doing so may limit Site functionality.

9. ANALYTICS AND MONITORING

We may use analytics tools to understand how the Site is used, detect security issues, and improve performance.

These tools may collect device and usage data but are not intended for behavioral advertising unless separately stated.

10. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal and regulatory requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the applicable obligations.

11. DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and you acknowledge that transmission of information over the internet involves inherent risks.

12. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER U.S. LAWS

Depending on your state of residence, including California, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion of certain information, request information about how your data is used, and opt out of certain forms of processing where applicable.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.
